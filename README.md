# Study Planner Template

A weekly study planner that actually gets used

## Overview
The Study Planner Template is a digital weekly layout designed for students who buy productivity tools but abandon them by Wednesday. It strips away complicated dashboards and aesthetic fluff to focus strictly on what you need to get through the week without burning out.

## Use cases
- Surviving finals week when you have overlapping assignments and zero idea where to start.
- Catching up after falling behind on readings because your schedule felt totally overwhelming.
- Balancing a heavy course load with a part-time job or extracurriculars.

## How to use
1. Download the PDF or import the digital notebook file into your preferred app like GoodNotes, Notability, or print it out.
2. Spend 10 minutes on Sunday filling in your fixed commitments and dropping every upcoming deadline into the priority triage section.
3. Check your page once every morning to cross off completed tasks and adjust your focus for the day.

## What is included
- Weekly grid
- Priority triage
- Review checklist
